Privacy Policy

Small Deeds Goal Journal · Last updated: November 4, 2026

Small Deeds Goal Journal is an iPhone app: Progress diary without streaks. It has no user accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics or tracking code and no advertising. Small Deeds Goal Journal does not collect any data from you.

What stays on your device

Everything the app keeps — your settings and anything you choose to save in it — stays on your device and in your own device or iCloud backups, except what the Apple services below carry. We never receive, see or store any of it. Deleting the app removes it from your device.

Permissions

Small Deeds Goal Journal asks for these only when you use the feature that needs them:

  • Face ID — Face ID unlocks your journal when you turn the lock on.

What the app reads through them is processed on your device and never reaches us — what goes to Apple is said under Apple services below. You can turn any of them off in iOS Settings.

Apple services

iCloud. When you are signed in to iCloud, Small Deeds Goal Journal keeps what you save in it in sync through your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit), so your devices share it. Apple stores it under its own privacy policy; we never receive or see it. You can turn iCloud off for Small Deeds Goal Journal in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Small Deeds Goal Journal offers an optional in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store: we never receive your payment details or your Apple ID. The app only learns from Apple whether a purchase was made, to unlock it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
